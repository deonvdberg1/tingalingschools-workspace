# SALT & STATIC — Premise

**Format:** manga · RTL · B5 print (182×257mm) · English
**Genre:** coastal supernatural / quiet horror / coming-of-age
**Working length:** 5 chapters (≈40–50 pages each) — Chapter 1 is a self-contained hook.

## Logline
On the last night shift of a dying coastal radio station, a teenage board-operator
answers a call that has been ringing since the town's lighthouse went dark — thirty years ago.

## The one-line pitch
*A coming-of-age ghost story told through a radio signal: a girl who is the last person
still listening in a town that stopped broadcasting, and the voice that never signed off.*

## Themes
- **What a place keeps saying after everyone stops listening.**
- Memory vs. record: the station's tape archive is more honest than the town's memory.
- Inheritance: the frequency was *handed down* — Nandi inherits a job, a debt, a voice.
- Analogue things that refuse to die: valves, tape, light, radio.

## Why this shape
The whole chapter happens in one room and one night. It proves the studio's range
(mood, silence, close-ups, lettering restraint) without needing a big cast or location
spread. The hook lands on the last page.
