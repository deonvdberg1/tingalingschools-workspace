# SALT & STATIC — Arc Outline (5 chapters)

Draft by Ink, 2026-09-13. Structure only — Mr D can bend any of it.

## Ch1 — THE 04:40 *(self-contained hook)*
Last night shift before the station closes. 04:40. The dead line rings.
Nandi answers. A voice reads coordinates. The lighthouse on Kestrel Head — dark for
thirty years — burns again. The voice says her name. Gogga, in the doorway:
*"…you answered it."*

**Pages:** 6 (p01–p06). **Cast:** Nandi, Gogga, the Caller.

## Ch2 — THE REEL
The station archive. Gogga plays her the 1996 sign-off tape. On it: his own voice,
thirty years younger, calmly reading the same coordinates — and then *her* voice,
answering, on a reel recorded before she was born.

## Ch3 — KESTREL HEAD
They climb the headland in the rain. The lamp room is warm. Something up there has
been keeping the log going, one entry per year, in handwriting that gets younger.

## Ch4 — THE LOG
The keeper's final entry, now written in Nandi's own hand — dated 14 March 1996.
To erase it she has to make sure someone is still listening when the light goes out.

## Ch5 — SIGN-OFF
The station's last night. Nandi does not run. She signs off the frequency properly —
the thing nobody managed in 1996 — and the light goes dark *by choice*.
Umoya Bay wakes up to no signal, and no ghost.

## The long answer (if it goes past 5)
The town itself is the ghost: a place that kept broadcasting a grief it never named.
Nandi's arc is from "I want to leave" to "I want to be the one who signs off."
