# ASH & APPETITE — Bible (test chapter, proposal)

Draft by Ink, 2026-09-13. **Nothing is canon until Mr D says so.**
Register: *Jujutsu Kaisen* — dark urban supernatural, cursed energy, ranked sorcerers,
brutal action with a quiet cost. Original story, original cast.

## Logline
A first-year jujutsu sorcerer can copy any cursed technique — but every taste burns one of
her own memories. To kill a Grade 2 curse that feeds a neighbourhood what it's missing,
she has to eat its last memory. What she loses: her mother's face.

## One-line pitch
*Hunger is a curse. So is remembering.*

## World
- **Setting:** Umgeni Ward, Durban, South Africa. A jujutsu academy hidden inside a
  decommissioned grain silo. Sorcerers here practice the old art far from Japan's schools.
- **Curses** are born from pooled human negativity — hunger, regret, grief. They feed on
  what people refuse to feel. The more a ward ignores its unhappiness, the stronger they get.
- **Cursed energy:** everyone leaks it; sorcerers are the ones who can shape it.
- **Grades (sorcerers):** 4 → 3 → 2 → 1 → Special. **Grades (curses):** the reverse danger curve.
  A Grade 2 curse needs a Grade 2 sorcerer minimum. Send a weaker one and you're sending meat.
- **The toll:** every cursed technique has a cost. That cost is the story.

## Cast
> Character blocks below are pasted **verbatim** into every art prompt (house rule).

**AYA NTOMBELA — 16 — Grade 3 sorcerer, first-year.**
Guarded, quick, dry-humoured. Keeps a written list of memories she still has, because she
doesn't trust her own head any more.
`AYA, a 16-year-old South African girl, dark brown skin, sharp black bob with a blunt fringe, a small silver ring in her left ear, dark school blazer over a white shirt with a loose red tie, narrow amber eyes, a chipped front tooth`

- **Cursed technique — "SECOND HELPING":** taste a cursed object, copy the technique bound to
  it. One taste = one technique. One technique = one memory, gone for good.

**GOOSE (Gideon Baloyi) — 30s — Special Grade, and he knows it.**
Sunny, unserious, terrifying when he stops smiling. Wears sunglasses indoors because his
eyes see cursed energy too well. Trains the first-years because the ward is short on anyone.
`GOOSE, a tall South African jujutsu sorcerer in his thirties, messy white hair, black wraparound sunglasses worn indoors, long charcoal coat over a rumpled shirt, bandaged right hand, an easy grin`

**THE COOK — Grade 2 curse.**
Born from one man's grief and a street's hunger after a beloved ramen stand closed.
Wears a cracked porcelain smile. Feeds people the thing they miss most — and they never
wake up. Politeness as horror.
`THE COOK, a hulking cursed spirit wearing a stained chef apron and a cracked white porcelain mask painted with a smile, too many arms, steam rising from its shoulders`

## Art style (locked for this book)
Target: **as close to the JJK register as the free toolchain allows** — hand-drawn 2D shonen
manga, NOT semi-realistic render. Hard rule: no photographic/3D look, no realistic skin, no
depth-of-field.
```
hand-drawn Japanese manga panel, black and white, shonen battle manga, dark supernatural
action manga style, clean crisp ink linework with bold varied weight, sharp angular stylized
character design, large expressive anime eyes, simplified stylized nose and mouth, flat
cel-shaded gray screentone, solid pure black fills, heavy shadows, dynamic dutch angles,
speed lines, high contrast, strictly 2D pen-and-ink illustration
```
Negative prompt (always): `photorealistic, photograph, 3d render, cgi, hyperrealistic,
realistic skin texture, depth of field, bokeh, blurry, soft rendering, gradient shading,
airbrush, color, letters, text, watermark`.
Lettering: same compositor stack — Comic Neue (dialogue), Bangers (SFX).
