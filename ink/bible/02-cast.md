## NANDI MKHIZE — 17 — protagonist
Night-shift board operator at Radio Umoya. The last person on the coast who still
listens to the night. Keeps a school exercise book of every strange call she's ever
taken — she says it's "for the listeners," but nobody listens.

- Wants: to leave Umoya Bay. Fears: that leaving means forgetting.
- Flaw: she answers things she should leave ringing.
- Secret: she has heard 1215 call before, at 04:40, and told no one.

**Character block (verbatim):**
`NANDI, a 17-year-old South African girl, deep brown skin, short natural coils poking out from a red knit beanie, oversized charcoal hoodie with a peeling band logo, headset resting around her neck, tired amber-brown eyes, a small gap between her front teeth`

---

## SIPHO "GOGGA" DUBE — 60s — engineer / mentor
Kept the valve transmitter alive by hand for thirty years. Was on shift the night the
light died. Warm, superstitious, whistles when he's frightened. Knows exactly what
1215 is — and has spent thirty years making sure nobody answers it.

- Wants: to shut the station down *cleanly*. Fears: 04:40.
- Flaw: he protects Nandi by lying to her.
- The whistle on his cord: he stops whistling at 04:40. That's how you know.

**Character block (verbatim):**
`GOGGA, a wiry South African man in his sixties, grizzled grey stubble, faded navy harbour overalls over a cream cable-knit jersey, round spectacles taped at the bridge, ink-stained fingers, a brass whistle on a cord around his neck`

---

## THE CALLER / THE KEEPER — the voice
Never fully shown. Carried on 1215 kHz. Calm, polite, patient. Reads coordinates the
way a man reads a shopping list. In Chapter 1 he says only three things.

**Character block (verbatim):**
`THE CALLER, a voice with no body, heard through telephone static, no face, represented only by darkness and interference`
