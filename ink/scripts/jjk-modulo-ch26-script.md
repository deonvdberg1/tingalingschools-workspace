# JUJUTSU KAISEN ≡ (MODULO) — CHAPTER 26
## "AFTER THE BRIGHT FUTURE" (明るい未来のあとで)

*Fan continuation, written by Ink for Mr D. Private test work — not for commercial publication (JJK is Shueisha's IP).*

### CONTINUITY CARD — where this picks up
Chapter 25 ("A Bright Future") closed the Modulo arc like this:
- The **Ritual of Harmony** drained cursed energy from the world. Anyone born afterwards has almost none — on Earth *and* on Simuria.
- The Simurian twins **Maru** and **Cross** reconciled; the Rumelians chose to live alongside the Kalyans as family.
- **Megumi Fushiguro is dead.** **Nobara Kugisaki survived**, and grows old.
- **Yuji Itadori** remains as *the witness* — and the world's last reservoir of cursed energy, kept in reserve as a final weapon. It was revealed that when he dies, Yuji's body will become a **cursed object, like Sukuna's fingers**.
- Cursed spirits, it turned out, are vessels for souls the same "colour" as the Kalyans and Rumelians.

**Chapter 26 takes place twenty years later, in 2106.**
The world got its happy ending. That is the problem.

---

## PAGE 1
**P1 — extreme wide, establishing.** Tokyo, 2106. A clean morning. Glass towers, canal water so still it looks solid, cherry trees along the embankment. Not a trace of pressure, dread, or rot. It looks *finished*.
> **CAPTION (top-left):** Twenty years after the Harmony.

**P2 — wide, school corridor.** Musashino Municipal High. Kids in blazers, laughing. Along one wall: a glass museum case. Black velvet inside. A bone-white object on a stand.
> **CAPTION:** We keep our monsters in glass now.

**P3 — close-up, the case.** The label: *SORCERY — THE AGE THAT ENDED.* The object behind the glass is vaguely a knuckle. Very old. Very quiet.
> **CAPTION (lower-left):** This one is a relic. Grade unknown. Donated 2089.

**P4 — medium, from behind.** Students pass the case without looking. One girl stops. **HINATA SAKAGUCHI**, 15, bag on both shoulders. She tilts her head at the case like it's speaking.
> **HINATA (thought):** …Why is that box full of *sound?*

---

## PAGE 2
**P1 — medium, classroom.** A teacher at the board, hand raised, cheerful. The lesson slide reads *UNIT 4: THE CULLING GAMES.*
> **TEACHER:** —and sorcery is *history*, class. Nobody is born with cursed energy any more. That is not a loss. That is the whole point of the last hundred years.

**P2 — close, Hinata's hand.** Below the desk, she's drawing in her notebook. Not the lesson. Spirals. Hundreds of them, pressed into the page so hard the paper is bruised.

**P3 — extreme close-up, her eye.** A normal brown eye. And around the pupil, too faint to be a trick of light — a thin **third ring**, the colour of an oil film on water.

**P4 — medium, desk.** Her friend **KENTA** leans over, grinning.
> **KENTA:** You did the staring thing again. At the *relic*.
> **HINATA:** It stares back, Ken.
> **KENTA:** It's a rock in a box.
> **HINATA:** Then why does it *hum?*

---

## PAGE 3
**P1 — wide, night.** The same corridor, empty, alarm-red. The museum case stands alone under a flickering strip light.

**P2 — close, inside the glass.** The bone-white relic. A hairline crack opens across it. Then another. From the cracks, something dark seeps — slow, deliberate, wrong, like ink in cold water.
> **SFX (small):** *tkn… tkn…*

**P3 — medium.** Hinata at the end of the corridor in pyjamas and a hoodie, phone-torch up, bare feet on cold tile. She came back. She should not have.
> **HINATA:** I'm not crazy. I heard it from my *house.*

**P4 — close, the case.** The crack is wider now. Something on the other side of the glass presses back — an impression of a hand with too many joints.
> **CAPTION (bottom):** Nobody else heard anything. That is the part that frightens her later.

---

## PAGE 4
**P1 — medium.** The glass fogs from the inside. The relic has grown a **tendril** — a thin root of black, feeling along the velvet.

**P2 — wide.** Outside the case, on the tile, a figure assembles itself out of soot and shadow. Incomplete. Wrong. A **curse** — or the shape a curse would take if you'd never seen one and only knew the word. It has no face.
> **SFX (large, jagged):** *KRRRRK*

**P3 — extreme close.** The blank face *turns toward Hinata anyway.* Where a mouth should be, thin black strokes arrange themselves, unhurried:
> **THE THING (jagged black lettering, no bubble):** h u n g r y

**P4 — close.** Hinata does not run. Her hand comes up, flat, like she's stopping traffic.
> **HINATA:** You're not supposed to be *real.* You're supposed to be *history.*

---

## PAGE 5
**P1 — wide, morning, an office.** A shopfront: **OKKOTSU CURSE DISPOSAL — RELICS & RESIDUE.** Potted plants, a sleeping cat, thirty years of dust on the filing. It looks like a tax accountant's office. It used to be the most dangerous address in Japan.

**P2 — medium.** **TSURUGI OKKOTSU**, early seventies, silver hair, a coat over his pyjamas, face-down on the desk, snoring.

**P3 — medium.** A phone rings. **YUKA OKKOTSU** — his sister, same age, sharper, coffee in one hand — answers without looking.
> **YUKA:** Okkotsu. …A relic's awake. Musashino Municipal High. Yes. *Awake.* You owe me for the next ten years for waking you, brother.

**P4 — close.** On the wall behind her: photographs. Two old black-and-white portraits — a man with a sword and kind eyes; a woman with a scar and a grin. Between them, a third frame turned **face-down.**

---

## PAGE 6
**P1 — close.** Tsurugi's hand on the desk. Scarred, stiff, knuckles too big. He flexes it; it doesn't want to.
> **TSURUGI (small, tired):** There hasn't been a curse on this side of the water in twenty years.
> **YUKA (off):** Then it isn't a curse.

**P2 — wide.** The door. **MARU** — old now, spine curved, a cloth band sealed over the **third eye** — shuffles in, leaning on a cane. Even with the band on, he aims his face exactly at the window, exactly at the school.
> **MARU:** It is a colour I have only heard described once. The one they said could never be made again.

**P3 — close, Yuka's face.** For the first time this morning, she is afraid.
> **YUKA:** …Then who is making it?

---

## PAGE 7
**P1 — wide, night, school.** Tsurugi and Yuka arrive. Police tape. The corridor is colder than outside. The case is still there. The **tendril has reached the floor.**

**P2 — medium.** Hinata, still barefoot, is standing between the thing and a huddle of students who came to film it. She's holding them back with one arm. She hasn't run in twenty minutes.

**P3 — close.** Tsurugi sees her. Sees what she's looking at. Sees that she *can* see it.
> **TSURUGI (thought):** …She's looking at it. Not *at* the noise. At *it.* The way we used to.

**P4 — medium.** The thing lunges. Tsurugi moves — a body that was once the strongest thing in the city, now slow, and hating it. He slaps a **talisman** onto the tile.
> **SFX:** *SHHHA—*
> **TSURUGI:** I am *too old* for this sentence to make sense.

---

## PAGE 8
**P1 — wide.** The talisman flares — and fizzles, weak, twenty years out of practice. Tsurugi is thrown back into a vending machine. Cans spray.

**P2 — close.** The thing doesn't chase him. It turns back to the **case.** It isn't hunting students. It's *kneeling* to the relic, the way a dog kneels to a door.

**P3 — close.** Hinata, understanding something nobody else can:
> **HINATA:** It's not hungry for *us.* It's hungry for *that.* It wants back in the box.

**P4 — medium.** Maru, at the end of the hall, lifting the band off his third eye for the first time in a decade. His eye is clouded, dying — and it weeps anyway.
> **MARU:** Then someone has opened it.

---

## PAGE 9
**P1 — extreme wide, cut to: underground.** Not the school. Somewhere deep and dry and old — the sub-basement of the **ruins of Jujutsu High.** A corridor of concrete, blast doors, and seals painted floor to ceiling, so old they've been repainted a hundred times, each layer a different prayer.

**P2 — medium.** A guard station. Two attendants — a young woman and an old man — watch a wall of monitors. Every monitor shows the same room.

**P3 — wide, the vault.** One room. One chair. One man.

**P4 — close.** **YUJI ITADORI.** He must be in his nineties. Small now, white-haired, IV lines and paper seals taped in spirals down his arms. He is asleep, and he has been asleep for years, and the seals on his body are *thrumming* — a slow, patient light, like a heart that isn't his.

> **CAPTION (lower-left):** THE LAST RESORT.
> **CAPTION (lower-right):** A promise nobody wanted to keep.

---

## PAGE 10
**P1 — close, monitor.** A hairline crack in one of the seals on Yuji's arm. Exactly like the crack in the relic. Identical. Mirror-image.

**P2 — medium, guard station.** The old attendant stands so fast his chair falls.
> **OLD ATTENDANT:** Wake the Okkotsus. Wake *everybody* who's left.
> **YOUNG ATTENDANT:** There's nobody left to wake. Sir — sir, after the Harmony there was *nobody left to wake.*

**P3 — close.** The old man looks at the monitor. Yuji sleeps on, and the seals glow a little brighter, as if something inside him has turned over in his sleep.
> **OLD ATTENDANT:** …Then wake the man in the chair.

---

## PAGE 11
**P1 — wide, the school corridor.** Tsurugi, Yuka and Maru around the thing, which has gone still — frozen mid-kneel, listening to something none of them can hear. Hinata can. Her eyes are wide.

**P2 — close, Tsurugi and Yuka.**
> **TSURUGI:** It's reaching for the reserve. The thing under the school. It's trying to *drink* him.
> **YUKA:** Then we seal it. Same as always.
> **MARU (off):** You cannot seal a thirst.

**P3 — medium, Maru.** He looks at his own hand, then at Yuji's distant, absent direction.
> **MARU:** The reserve is not a battery. It is a *person.* And he has been holding the last of it inside his body for twenty years so that no one would ever have to. If that thing drinks him — it doesn't get *power.* It gets *him.*

**P4 — close, Hinata, quietly.**
> **HINATA:** Then don't let it.

---

## PAGE 12
**P1 — medium.** Everyone turns to the fifteen-year-old in pyjamas.
> **TSURUGI:** Girl. Go home.
> **HINATA:** I *saw* it. Before it existed. In the afternoon, in a *glass box*, I *saw* it. You didn't. Can you do that?
> **TSURUGI (after a beat):** …No.

**P2 — close.** Hinata lifts her chin.
> **HINATA:** Then I'm already in this, and you're *old*, and I'm the only one who can find the door.

**P3 — close, Maru.** He has gone very still. He is looking at Hinata with the specific terror of a man recognising an old friend's handwriting.
> **MARU (thought):** …She has a *colour.* She was born after the Harmony. She should have none.

**P4 — wide.** Yuka steps between them, coat over her arm, suddenly the only adult in the room.
> **YUKA:** Enough. Nobody's drinking anybody. We go to the vault. We look at it with our eyes. Then we decide.

---

## PAGE 13
**P1 — wide, the underground corridor.** Four of them and a fifteen-year-old, walking toward the vault. Hinata's bare feet on old concrete. The seals on the walls glow faintly as she passes — *more* faintly than they should, as if she's drinking them a little, or warming them.

**P2 — close.** Hinata's palm, raised. As she walks, the oil-film colour spreads under her skin, like a river finding its banks.

**P3 — close, Tsurugi watching her hand.** He knows that colour. He saw it once, in a photograph of a man named Kenjaku who wore other people's faces.
> **TSURUGI (thought):** …No. Not that. Something *new.* Something the world made because it *had to.*

**P4 — medium.** Ahead: the vault door. Open. The guards, unconscious. The chair, empty.

---

## PAGE 14
**P1 — wide.** The chair is empty. The IV lines hang slack. The seals on the floor spell a circle that has been *stepped out of* — walked through, from the inside.

**P2 — medium.** And there, at the far wall, is **Yuji.** Standing. Upright. Small and old and *awake.* He has his back to them. He is looking at the wall as if it were a window.

**P3 — close.** The relic's tendril has found its way here — it's curled around the IV stand, drinking the drip. Yuji is holding the tendril's tip in one hand, gently, the way you'd hold a kitten's paw.

**P4 — close, Yuji's face, half-turned.**
> **YUJI (quiet, and kind, and *very* tired):** Ah. There you are. I was starting to think I'd fall back asleep before somebody came.

---

## PAGE 15
**P1 — close.** Tsurugi's face — recognition and grief at once.
> **TSURUGI:** Itadori. You're supposed to be *sealed.*
> **YUJI:** I *am* sealed. That's the trouble. Sealed things *hold.* A seal that holds for twenty years is a seal that's been *full* for twenty years.

**P2 — medium.** Yuji turns. He is *cracked.* Cracks run under his skin like the cracks in the relic — the first hairline of a body beginning to become a cursed object.
> **YUJI:** It's not a curse that got out, kids. It's *me* that's getting out. Bit by bit. That's what a cursed object *is.* That's what I was always going to be.

**P3 — close, Yuka.**
> **YUKA:** Then we reseal you. Deeper.
> **YUJI:** And in forty years, when the seal leaks again — a *bigger* bit of me gets out. You're not stopping it. You're *rationing* it.

**P4 — wide.** Yuji looks past all of them, at the one person he hasn't seen yet. Hinata. And Hinata's eyes have gone wide — because standing here, close, she can see that the colour she's been staring at her whole life is the same colour leaking out of him.
> **YUJI:** …Oh. *You can see it too, then.*

---

## PAGE 16
**P1 — extreme close.** Hinata's eye. The third ring, unmistakable now, the colour of oil on water. And reflected in it: Yuji, cracked, patient.

**P2 — close.** Maru, from behind them, very softly:
> **MARU:** The Harmony did not remove the cursed energy. It *moved* it. It put it somewhere the world couldn't hurt itself with it any more. Somewhere with a *shape.*
> **MARU:** And now it has waited twenty years to be *born* by children who were told they had none.

**P3 — close, Hinata.** She looks at her own hands like she's never met them.
> **HINATA:** …I'm not a person with no cursed energy.
> **HINATA:** I'm a person *full of it*, and nobody thought to write that down.

**P4 — wide, Yuji.** He smiles — the same easy, stupid smile from a hundred years and a thousand fights ago.
> **YUJI:** Good. Then I'm not the last resort any more. I'm just the *last guy who volunteered.*

---

## PAGE 17
**P1 — wide.** The vault. The tendril finally dies, starved, evaporating into steam, because nobody is feeding it any more. The crisis is over.

**P2 — medium.** But as it dies, far above them, in the school, the empty museum case — the glass fogs. Just once. Something presses back. Something *patient.*

**P3 — wide, exterior.** Tokyo, 2106, pre-dawn. The city is clean and quiet and finished. And in the sky over the water — visible to exactly one fifteen-year-old, and to no one else — a slow colour spreads across the horizon like a stain in a developing photograph.
> **CAPTION:** The world without curses lasted exactly twenty years.

**P4 — extreme close, Hinata's eye, last panel.** The third ring. Wide open.

---

## PAGE 18 — epilogue beat
**P1 — medium.** The Okkotsu office, morning. The cat. The kettle. Tsurugi asleep at the desk again, face-down, coat over his head.

**P2 — close.** Yuka hangs up the phone. She looks at the photo wall — the two portraits, and the third frame, still face-down. She turns it, finally. It's a photograph of four friends at a school gate: a boy with a kind face, a girl with a scar, a boy with a topknot, and Yuji Itadori, laughing, holding up two fingers.

**P3 — close.** The telephone rings again. Yuka watches it ring. Even here — even now — her hand moves to answer it.

**P4 — wide.** Outside, the city. Ordinary. And walking through it, a fifteen-year-old girl in a school blazer, barefoot no longer, looking up at a colour nobody else can see — and walking *toward* it, not away.

> **CAPTION (bottom-right):** CHAPTER 26 — END

### NOTES FOR THE STUDIO (not part of the chapter)
- **Thematic spine:** every JJK arc is *power has a price*. Here the price of the world's peace is that the price was *deferred* — moved into children who were promised it wasn't there. Yuji's ending line reframes the "last resort" from tragedy into a choice.
- **What I deliberately did not do:** resurrect Megumi, undo the Harmony, or bring back Sukuna. A continuation should *inherit* the ending, not overwrite it.
- **Art direction if drawn:** Yuji's cracks mirror the relic's cracks (same motif, page 3 ↔ page 15). Hinata's colour is the only non-black element in the whole chapter — keep it a single flat tone, never rendered, never explained visually.
