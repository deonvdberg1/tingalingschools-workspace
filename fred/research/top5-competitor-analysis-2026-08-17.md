## 3. Prioritised improvement list for autoeffortless.com

### 🔴 P1 — High impact, low effort (do now)
1. **Live demo CTA in the hero** — "Try it: message 061 527 4429 on WhatsApp and watch it reply" — the #1 thing no competitor can copy. Turns the static mockup into a real test drive.
2. **Trust badges strip** — "Official WhatsApp Business API (Meta) · CIPC registered · POPIA compliant · 24/7 monitored, hourly backups" — all true, all ours.
3. **Thicken the "Pricing on request" card** — add "What's included" list (setup handled, WhatsApp API approval managed, 24/7 monitoring, monthly report, human handoff, local support) so the section isn't empty. Keep "on request" per your call.
4. **Expand FAQ 5 → 9** — add: POPIA/privacy, multilingual (English/Afrikaans/isiZulu), how handoff to a human works, what "on request" pricing depends on, do we need a WhatsApp Business account, uptime/monitoring.
5. **WhatsApp floating chat widget** — small green bubble bottom-right on every screen → wa.me link. Standard everywhere; takes 10 lines.
6. **Footer legal links** — Privacy Policy + POPIA page (already drafted in our workspace!) — builds trust and is legally sensible.

### 🟡 P2 — Medium effort (this month)
7. **Outcome section instead of removed stats** — honest numbers we can actually stand behind once we have a few paying months: e.g., "Every enquiry answered in under 60 seconds" (true for the product) and "No lead left unanswered — ever". Frame as product truths, not marketing fiction.
8. **Industry lanes** — 3 short cards: "For Schools", "For Churches", "For Local Businesses" with one concrete example each (no client names). Mirrors Wati's segmentation.
9. **"Why not a DIY platform?" section** — the differentiator play: *"Wati/ManyChat give you a dashboard and a learning curve. We give you a working assistant — we build it, run it, and fix it. No tech skills. One phone number for your customers."* Directly answers the comparison buyers make.
10. **Integrations section** — WhatsApp, Instagram, Google Calendar, Stripe payment links, Gmail, website forms (all real).
11. **JSON-LD LocalBusiness schema + sitemap.xml** — free SEO; we rank for "WhatsApp assistant Richards Bay" with zero competition.
12. **Case-study page (anonymous)** — "School in Richards Bay: 100% of parent enquiries answered 24/7, PDF statements on demand" — metrics we actually have from Ting-A-Ling, no names.

### 🟢 P3 — Strategic (next quarter)
13. **Multi-page structure** — dedicated /for-schools, /for-churches, /pricing, /faq, /contact pages (SEO + conversion; all competitors are multi-page).
14. **Blog** — 2 posts/month on WhatsApp-for-business in SA; feeds SEO and the parent-lead campaign content.
15. **Free-trial / pilot offer** — "Free 7-day pilot for schools" (your call — pricing decision).
16. **Afrikaans version** — given NG Kerk is Afrikaans-first; Wati/AiSensy localize in 10+ languages; SA bilingual is a genuine edge.
17. **Testimonials** — collect 2–3 short quotes from Ting-A-Ling/NG Kerk contacts (with permission) once they've been live longer.
