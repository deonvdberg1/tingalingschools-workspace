# AutoEffortless — Sales Playbook for Mr D

## How The Business Works (Simple Version)

**We sell a managed AI assistant for WhatsApp and Instagram.**

The AI answers customer messages automatically — 24/7, instantly, in natural language. If the AI can't handle something, it hands off to the business owner.

We set it up, train it on their business, and monitor it. They pay us monthly. We do all the tech work.

## How To Approach Someone

### Option A: Warm Introduction (Best)
You know them. You message them:
> *"Hey [Name], I'm working with an AI automation company on something interesting. We set up AI assistants for businesses on WhatsApp — handles customer enquiries automatically. Would it be worth 10 minutes to show you what it looks like?"*

### Option B: Walk-In (Second Best)
You visit their business. Ask to speak to the owner/manager.
> *"Hi, I'm helping local businesses automate their WhatsApp. Takes about 10 minutes to explain. Do you have a moment or should I come back?"*

### Option C: WhatsApp Cold Message
> *"Hi [Name], I noticed you get a lot of messages on WhatsApp. Do you ever struggle to keep up with replies? I've been helping businesses set up AI assistants that handle customer enquiries automatically. Worth a quick chat?"*

## The Pitch Call (What To Say)

### Opening (30 seconds)
> *"I run an AI service called Fred AI. We set up automated assistants for businesses on WhatsApp. Basically, when a customer messages you, the AI replies instantly — answers common questions, books appointments, captures leads. Only hands things to you when it can't handle it. It runs 24/7, even when you're closed."*

### The Problem Check (30 seconds)
> *"Do you find yourself busy during the day and then have 50 WhatsApp messages you haven't replied to? Or do you get messages on a Sunday that you only see on Monday?"*

### The Solution (30 seconds)
> *"That's exactly what we solve. The AI replies instantly. Your customers get help in seconds, not hours. And you only get involved for the important stuff."*

### Price & Close (30 seconds)
> *"Setup is R[5/7/12]k depending on complexity. Then R[3/5/8]k per month. That covers everything — setup, training, management, monitoring. Most businesses make that back in their first week from one or two extra sales they would have missed."*

### If They Say Yes
> *"Great. Let me send you a one-pager with what we discussed. Then we'll schedule a 30-minute discovery call to learn about your business. After that, we set everything up in about a week."*

### If They Say "Not Now"
> *"No problem. Can I send you a one-pager so you have it? If things change, just let me know."*

### If They Say "I'll Think About It"
> *"Sure, take your time. One thing I'd suggest — set a reminder to check in a week. Most business owners I talk to realise they've lost a few leads in that week that the AI would have caught. But no pressure."*

## Questions You'll Get Asked (With Answers)

### "How does it actually work?"
> *"We connect it to your WhatsApp Business account. We train the AI on your business — what you sell, your hours, your pricing, common questions. Then when a customer messages, the AI responds automatically. It uses the same technology as ChatGPT, trained specifically on your business."*

### "What if it gives wrong information?"
> *"We monitor every conversation. If something goes wrong, we catch it and fix it. You also get a weekly report showing everything that happened. And the AI only answers what we've trained it on — if a question is out of scope, it hands off to you."*

### "Will my customers know it's AI?"
> *"Some might guess, but most won't care because they get an instant answer. If a customer asks 'is this a bot?', the AI can honestly say 'I'm an AI assistant trained to help you, but [business owner name] is here if you need them.'"*

### "How is this different from the free WhatsApp Business app?"
> *"WhatsApp Business has preset replies you can type yourself. This is an AI that understands context, answers naturally, books appointments into your calendar, and qualifies leads — all automatically. It's a completely different level."*

### "What happens if WhatsApp blocks me?"
> *"We handle the official WhatsApp Business API approval. Your number is verified and compliant. We've never had a block. Plus, we include backup channels."*

### "Can I stop anytime?"
> *"Yes. Month-to-month. You just give us notice. No lock-in contracts."*

### "How long does setup take?"
> *"About a week. The WhatsApp approval takes a few days, then training and testing takes a few more days. You're involved for a 30-minute discovery call and a 30-minute test session."*

### "Can I see it working on MY phone?"
> *"After setup, we give you test access. You message your own WhatsApp number and see it reply. If you're happy, we go live."*

## What NOT To Say

- ❌ "You need AI to survive" — fear-based, sounds desperate
- ❌ "I'll do it for you myself" — you're not technical, I am
- ❌ Complicated tech terms (API, webhook, LLM, training data)
- ❌ "How much are you spending on lost leads?" — too aggressive
- ❌ Promise specific numbers ("you'll make R50k more") — you don't know their business
