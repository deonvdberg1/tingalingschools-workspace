The Model

  • Pure web applications — hosted by us, used in the browser. No apps to install.
  • Click and buy — Stripe checkout → account auto-created → login → self-serve setup wizard.
  • Zero work from our side per sale — the customer does their own setup inside the app.
  • No WhatsApp / Meta / verification-heavy integrations — deliberately excluded.
  • Two price shapes — monthly subscription (Stripe auto-renew) or one-time lifetime access.
