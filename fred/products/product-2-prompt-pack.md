- **Price:** R99 ($5 USD)

- **Tagline:** Stop typing "write me a post" — get prompts that actually work for South African businesses.

## Product Description

Most prompt packs are written by Americans, for Americans. "Write a post for Small Business Saturday" doesn't help when you're in Durban.

This pack contains 50 prompts written specifically for South African business owners, in South African English, with local context.
