## Product Description

South African businesses are losing leads every day because they can't answer WhatsApp messages fast enough.

This blueprint shows you exactly how to set up an AI-powered WhatsApp assistant for your business — the same system we use for our clients, in a DIY format.

### What's Inside (14 Pages)
1. **WhatsApp Business vs WhatsApp Business API** — What you need and when
2. **The 3-Tool Stack** — Free and low-cost tools that work together
3. **Step-by-Step Setup Guide** — From zero to running in under an hour
4. **Business FAQ Template** — Fill this in and your AI knows everything
5. **Response Scripts for 10 Common Scenarios** — Pricing, hours, booking, complaints
6. **The Handover Protocol** — When to hand off to a human
7. **Monitoring Checklist** — How to make sure it's working

### Who This Is For
- Solopreneurs
- Small shop owners
- Service providers (salons, clinics, consultants)
- Anyone drowning in WhatsApp messages

### Who This Is NOT For
- Large businesses needing custom integrations
- Anyone who doesn't want to tinker with settings

### What You Need
- A WhatsApp Business account (free)
- 1 hour of focused time
- Basic comfort with following step-by-step instructions
