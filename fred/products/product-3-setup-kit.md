## Product Description

An AI assistant isn't magic — it's a system. This kit gives you the system, pre-built and ready to go.

### What You Get

**1. The Setup Guide (PDF — 22 pages)**
- How AI assistants actually work (simple explanation)
- The 5-step setup process
- Choosing the right tools for your budget (free options included)
- How to train your AI on YOUR business
- Testing and quality control checklist
- Troubleshooting guide

**2. Business Knowledge Base Template (Google Sheets)**
- Pre-built spreadsheet with prompts for every part of your business
- Sections for: Products/Services, FAQ, Pricing, Policies, Hours
- Fill it in once, your AI knows everything

**3. Notion AI Assistant Dashboard (Template)**
- Optional — for Notion users
- Pre-built workspace with:
  - Customer conversation log
  - Weekly performance tracker
  - Response quality checklist
  - Handover protocol
  - Learning log (what the AI got wrong and how to fix it)

**4. 10 Done-for-You Workflow Recipes**
- Lead capture → Follow-up sequence
- Appointment booking → Confirmation → Reminder
- Customer support → Escalation to human
- Payment follow-up sequence
- New customer onboarding
- Quote request → Follow-up → Close
- +4 more
