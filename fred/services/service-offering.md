### ✅ 24/7 Operation
Your AI assistant works:

- 11 PM on a Sunday

When the AI can't handle something — it tags you. You jump in, handle it, and the AI learns for next time.

## How It Works (The 7-Day Setup)

### Day 1: Discovery Call (30 min)
- You tell us about your business
- What questions do customers ask most?
- What hours do you operate?
- Do you have a calendar booking system?

### Day 2: We Build
- We set up the WhatsApp Business API
- We train the AI on your business
- We connect your calendar
- We test everything internally

### Day 3: You Test
- We give you access to the test system
- You send test messages and see how it responds
- You tell us what to tweak

### Day 4: Refine
- We make changes based on your feedback
- We test again

### Days 5-7: Soft Launch
- AI goes live but we monitor every conversation
- We fix anything that's not perfect
- You get comfortable with the weekly report

### Day 8+: Full Operation
- AI runs fully autonomously
- You get your weekly report
- We handle any issues
