Privacy Policy

AutoEffortless (Pty) Ltd — Effective: 28 May 2026

About this document: This Privacy Policy explains how AutoEffortless (Pty) Ltd ("AutoEffortless", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information when providing our services. We are a registered company in South Africa and comply with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

1. Who We Are

AutoEffortless (Pty) Ltd is a South African company that provides AI-powered communication services for businesses. We operate as a data processor — our clients (businesses) are the data controllers who determine what personal information is collected and why.

Contact: info@autoeffortless.com

2. What Personal Information We Process

We process the following categories of personal information on behalf of our clients:

  • Contact details: Phone numbers, names, and profile information from messaging platforms (WhatsApp, Instagram)
  • Communication data: Message content, timestamps, and interaction history between businesses and their customers
  • Usage data: Message volumes, response times, and engagement metrics for service improvement
  • Business information: Client company names, contact details, and billing information

3. How We Collect Information

Information is collected through:

  • Direct messaging: When customers send messages to our clients' WhatsApp business numbers or social media accounts
  • Client setup: When businesses register for our services and provide their company details
  • Automated systems: Our AI agents process messages to provide automated responses

4. Purpose of Processing

We process personal information solely for:

  • Operating AI-powered communication assistants for our clients
  • Responding to enquiries on behalf of our clients
  • Improving our AI systems and service quality
  • Billing and account management
  • Complying with legal obligations

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

5. Data Storage and Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information:

  • Encryption in transit: All data transmitted through our systems is encrypted via HTTPS and Cloudflare's secure tunnel
  • Access control: Only authorised personnel (the client and AutoEffortless administrators) have access to conversation data
  • Server security: Our systems run on a dedicated, password-protected server with no public remote access
  • Regular backups: Data is backed up hourly to encrypted repositories

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary:

  • Conversation data: Retained for 90 days after the last message, then automatically and permanently deleted
  • Client account data: Retained for the duration of the service agreement plus 12 months for legal compliance
  • Billing records: Retained for 5 years as required by South African tax law

7. Data Subject Rights (POPIA)

Individuals whose personal information we process have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal information held in our systems
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information
  • Objection: Object to the processing of your personal information
  • Data portability: Request transfer of your data to another service provider

To exercise these rights, contact the business you were communicating with (the data controller), or email us at info@autoeffortless.com with the business name and your phone number. We will respond within 7 business days.

8. Automated Decision-Making

Our AI agents make automated decisions in the form of responses to customer enquiries. These decisions are based strictly on the knowledge base provided by our clients. If you believe an automated decision is incorrect or wish to speak to a human, ask the AI agent — it will provide the relevant contact information.

9. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, each with their own privacy practices:

  • Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform): Message transmission. Meta's privacy policy applies to message delivery.
  • Meta (Instagram): Message transmission via Instagram Direct.
  • Cloudflare: Secure tunnel and DNS services.
  • DeepSeek: AI model provider for generating automated responses.
  • GitHub: Code repository and backup storage.

10. Security Breach Procedure

In the event of a data breach involving personal information, we will:

  • Notify the Information Regulator within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach
  • Notify affected data subjects (our clients) immediately
  • Provide details of the breach, potential impact, and remedial actions
  • Investigate and implement measures to prevent recurrence
