Privacy Policy

D&S Comp (trading as Ting-A-Ling Schools) — Effective: 14 May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how D&S Comp, trading as Ting-A-Ling Schools ("we", "us", or "our"), collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information when you communicate with us via WhatsApp or other messaging channels.

1. Information We Collect

When you contact us via WhatsApp, we may collect:

  • Contact information: Your phone number, name, and profile information you share
  • Communication data: Messages, questions, requests, and any information you voluntarily provide
  • Child-related information: If you are a parent or guardian, information about your child (name, grade, class) that you share with us for school-related purposes
  • Usage data: Message timestamps, response times, and interaction patterns to improve our service

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely for:

  • Responding to your enquiries about school fees, hours, enrolment, events, and other school matters
  • Communicating important school announcements and updates
  • Processing absentee reports and other school administrative matters
  • Improving our automated communication systems

3. Data Storage & Security

  • Conversations are stored securely and are only accessible to authorised school staff
  • WhatsApp messages are end-to-end encrypted between your device and WhatsApp's servers
  • We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data
  • Data is retained only as long as necessary for school communication purposes

4. Automated Responses

When you message us on WhatsApp, an automated system may respond to common queries. If your query requires human attention, it is escalated to a school staff member. Automated responses are clearly marked and you can request to speak to a human at any time by asking.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Meta (WhatsApp): Messages are processed through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform. Meta's privacy policy applies to the transmission of your messages
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your information
  • Opt out of automated communications at any time
  • Request a copy of your conversation history

To exercise these rights, contact us at the details below.
