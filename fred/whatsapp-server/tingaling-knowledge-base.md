## 🏫 SCHOOL OVERVIEW

| School | Address | Ages |
|--------|---------|:----:|

## 🧒 PRE-PRIMARY SCHOOL

- **Ages:** 2 to 6 years
- **Approach:** Play-based learning
- **Language:** English language nurturing integrated into daily programme
- **School readiness programme** for children transitioning to primary school
- **Focus areas:** Building confidence, creativity, and school readiness
- **Half-day options available**
- Small class sizes for individual attention

- Small, focused class groups

  - Parent/guardian ID
  - Latest school report (if applicable)

- **Registration fee applies**
- **Enrolment form has 7 sections:**
  1. Student Information
  2. Parent Information
  3. Emergency Contacts
  4. Medical Information
  5. Pick-up Authorisation
  6. Fees & Consent
  7. Terms & Signature

## ❓ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**Q: Can I visit the school before enrolling?**
A: Yes. Contact the office to arrange a tour during school hours.

**Q: Do you offer half-day options?**
A: Yes, half-day options are available at the Pre-Primary School.

**Q: Is there aftercare available?**
A: Yes, aftercare is available until 17:00 on school days.

**Q: Do you have holiday programmes?**
A: Yes, holiday programmes run during school breaks.

**Q: What therapy services are available?**
A: The Special Needs School offers therapy support including occupational.

**Q: How do I pay school fees?**
A: Via EFT, Debit Order, Cash, or other arranged method. Fees are due by the 2nd of each month.
