### Appointment

In accordance with Section 55 of the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), Act 4 of 2013, I hereby appoint you as the **Information Officer** of AutoEffortless (Pty) Ltd.

### Responsibilities

As Information Officer, you are responsible for:

1. **POPIA Compliance:** Ensuring the company processes personal information in accordance with POPIA
2. **Data Subject Requests:** Handling requests from individuals to access, correct, or delete their personal information
3. **Training:** Promoting awareness of POPIA requirements within the company
4. **Breach Response:** Managing data breach procedures, including notification to the Information Regulator
5. **Registration:** Maintaining the company's registration with the Information Regulator
6. **Record Keeping:** Maintaining a record of all processing activities
7. **Cooperation:** Acting as the contact point for the Information Regulator on all POPIA-related matters

### Acceptance

I accept the appointment as Information Officer of AutoEffortless (Pty) Ltd and confirm my understanding of the responsibilities outlined above.
