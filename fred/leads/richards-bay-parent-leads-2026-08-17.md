## ⚠️ The honest reality (read first)

**Individual parent names + phone numbers are NOT publicly available to scrape** in South Africa. POPIA (Protection of Personal Information Act) makes it illegal to harvest and use private individuals' contact details for marketing without consent, and buying "parent databases" from list vendors is both unlawful and full of fake data. I won't fabricate a list — that would burn the school's reputation.

**What I CAN give you:** every organisation, venue, group and professional in the area where parents of 0–6 year olds and special needs children already are — with verified contact numbers — plus the exact outreach play to reach them. This is the real, sustainable lead engine. It also doubles as Ting-A-Ling's referral partner network.

## 6️⃣ OUTREACH PLAYBOOK (compliant, POPIA-safe)

1. **Referral partnerships (Week 1–2):** Call Able Centre, Archways, Zululand Remedial, Little Rascals, Thuthukani. Offer: overflow placements, joint open day, cross-referrals. Goal: they recommend Ting-A-Ling to parents they can't take.
2. **Facebook group presence (Week 1):** Join all 5 groups. Post an open-day invitation + a helpful guide ("What to look for in a pre-primary school / How to know if your child needs extra support") — value first, sell second. Engage daily.
3. **Zululand Observer (Week 2–3):** Advertorial/feature on the special needs school + waiting-list demand story. Also enter "Best of Zululand — Kids" category (Annie's Swim School won 1st place in Swim; schools feature heavily).
4. **Therapy/paediatrician flyers (Week 2):** Drop flyers + referral cards at Dr Duze, Netcare The Bay, Medicross, Melomed, Annie's Swim School, equestrian club, malls.
5. **Open day event (Week 3–4):** Host at the school; invite all partner organisations to attend; advertise via ZO + FB groups. Collect opt-in contacts (POPIA-compliant consent form — we already have POPIA docs drafted).
6. **WhatsApp channel:** Use the existing Ting-A-Ling WhatsApp (068 754 8390) with keyword campaigns (parents message "INFO") — every flyer/group post drives to one number.

## 7️⃣ WHAT'S BLOCKED (and why)

- **Individual parent names/numbers:** not legally scrapeable in SA (POPIA); any vendor selling them is selling harvested/illegal data. Skip — the playbook above reaches the same people through channels they trust.
- **Facebook member data:** FB ToS prohibits scraping member lists; groups also require membership.
- **Thuthukani waiting list (350+):** not published, but the school itself is the referral gate — partner with them, don't scrape them.
